RULES & REGULATIONS

To ensure a fair and elite EA FC Mobile arena for all competitors, the following rules are strictly enforced within Mystic Legends.

FAIR PLAY POLICY: Network manipulation (Lag-switching), VPN usage for advantage, or excessive toxicity will result in an immediate and permanent arena ban.

1. MATCHPLAY PROTOCOLS

Technical Disconnections: In the event of a disconnection before half-time (45'), the match must be replayed from 0-0. If a disconnect occurs after 70', the score at the time of exit stands as final.
Fair Play Mechanics: While skill moves are part of the game, deliberate exploitation of "Kick-off Glitches" or "Constant Cross-Spamming" (repeatedly using only crosses to score) may lead to a point deduction upon review of evidence.
Squad Requirements: Players must adhere to OVR caps or specific player type restrictions if specified in the tournament roster description.

2. REPORTING & EVIDENCE

  • Both Managers must take a screenshot of the "Match Summary" showing the goals and full stats.
  • Results must be uploaded to the Mystic Results Portal or WhatsApp channel within 15 minutes.
  • If a match is disputed, video evidence of the foul play or lag must be provided to the Admins.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

By registering for Mystic Legends tournaments, you accept the following governing terms.

1. ACCOUNT ELIGIBILITY

Only one account per Manager is permitted. Using a "Smurf" account or playing on behalf of another user is strictly prohibited and leads to immediate disqualification from the season.

2. PRIZE POOLS & PAYOUTS

Prize distributions are processed via the stated payment method (UPI, PayPal, or In-game Points) within 72 hours of the Tournament Final. Ensure your payout details are correct; Mystic Legends is not responsible for funds sent to incorrectly provided IDs.

3. COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY

Mystic Legends reserves the right to review any match. If a match is deemed "fixed" or if players are found to be colluding to manipulate the leaderboard, both players will be expelled.

PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy is respected within our mobile gaming community.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

We collect only necessary competitive data:

  • EA FC In-game Name (IGN) and User ID (UID).
  • WhatsApp Contact (for match pairing and alerts).
  • Historical performance data for our Global Rankings.

2. DATA SECURITY

Your contact details are encrypted and only accessible to Senior Admins for payout and coordination purposes. We never share your personal information with external advertisers.

3. IMAGE USAGE

By participating, you allow Mystic Legends to use your Profile Picture URL and IGN in our Leaderboards and Broadcast Center for community recognition.
